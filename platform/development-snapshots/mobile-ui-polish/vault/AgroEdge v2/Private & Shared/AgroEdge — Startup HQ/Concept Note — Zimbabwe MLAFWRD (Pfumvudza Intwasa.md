## Summary

AgroEdge proposes an AI farm-intelligence and credit-readiness layer that sits on top of the IDEA farmer registry and the Pfumvudza/Intwasa input scheme. It closes the gap between the millions of supported farmers and the small share currently digitised — turning farmer data into decisions, and giving the Ministry real-time visibility into input use and outcomes. Free to farmers; funded by the program or a development partner.

## The opportunity

Pfumvudza/Intwasa supports roughly 3.5 million households, while the IDEA registry currently reaches tens of thousands of farmers across a handful of districts, and registries remain fragmented (crops vs tobacco). The Ministry can see who receives inputs, but has limited insight into what happens next — usage, productivity, profitability, repayment potential. Without that layer, targeting, transparency, and the shift from subsidy to self-sufficiency stay difficult.

## The solution — AgroEdge

Interoperable with IDEA, AgroEdge adds:

- **Activity, expense and income logging** per farmer, captured with AGRITEX extension support.
- **Dashboards, AI insights and alerts** — cost optimisation, profitability, productivity recommendations, and timely pest alerts (sector evidence links timely pest alerts to higher smallholder yield and income).
- **Loan / insurance-readiness scoring** — turning farm records into a data trail that connects farmers to finance and de-risks input credit.

It complements IDEA and Pfumvudza; it does not replace the registry.

## Why this fits Zimbabwe now

- Aligns with the national agriculture-digitisation push and the Ministry's own request for better digital tools.
- Strengthens transparency and accountability in input delivery.
- Provides the value-added intelligence layer the registry currently lacks — the bridge from input distribution to farmer self-sufficiency.

## The model

Funded by the program or a development partner (e.g. AfDB under the Emergency Food Production Facility, FAO, or similar); free to farmers and AGRITEX officers. Given fiscal constraints, the realistic funding route is donor-led rather than direct Treasury spend.
