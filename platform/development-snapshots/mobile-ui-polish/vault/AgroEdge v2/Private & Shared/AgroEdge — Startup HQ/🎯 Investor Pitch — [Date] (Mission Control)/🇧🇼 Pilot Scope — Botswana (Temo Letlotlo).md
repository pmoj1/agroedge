# 🇧🇼 Pilot Scope — Botswana (Temo Letlotlo)

> One-page pilot scope. Pitched as a **self-funded Ministry pilot** sitting on top of the Temo Letlotlo Crop Management System. `[Brackets]` = fill before sending. Budget comes from AgroEdge's financial model.

## Objective

Prove that adding AgroEdge's AI intelligence layer to the Temo Letlotlo CMS measurably improves farmer-level decisions and gives the Ministry program-level visibility — in one district, one season, at low cost.

## What AgroEdge provides

- Farmer profiles synced/aligned with the CMS, plus activity, expense and income logging.
- Dashboard + AI insights (cost, profitability, productivity, alerts).
- Extension-officer training and a simple field workflow.
- A baseline→endline data report for the Ministry.

## What the Ministry provides

- Endorsement and permission to integrate with the CMS (within data rules).
- Access to extension officers in the pilot district.
- The pilot farmer cohort.
- A named program counterpart.
- Pilot funding.

1. **Weeks 0–2:** Sign-off, CMS integration, district + cohort selection.
2. **Weeks 2–4:** Extension-officer training, farmer onboarding.
3. **Season:** Logging, insights, mid-season check-in.
4. **Endline:** Data report, Ministry review, go/no-go for scale-up.

## Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| New government may rework Temo Letlotlo | Frame pilot as helping the new administration deliver its commercialisation goal |
| Connectivity gaps in district | Offline-tolerant logging; extension-officer-assisted capture |
| Low farmer turnout | Recruit through trusted extension officers; keep the cohort small and supported |
