# Go-to-Market

## Strategy

> **Phase 1:** Land a paid pilot with a government or national agricultural programme (e.g. Botswana's Temo Letlotlo) covering 20–50 farmers.
**Phase 2:** Use pilot outcomes and data to expand the contract, win adjacent national programmes, and raise pre-seed.
**Phase 3:** Scale across government programmes in priority markets, with SMS/USSD reach to enrolled farmers.
>
