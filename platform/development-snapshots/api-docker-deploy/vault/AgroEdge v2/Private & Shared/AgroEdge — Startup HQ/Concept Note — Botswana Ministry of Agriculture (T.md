## Summary

AgroEdge proposes to add an AI-powered farm-intelligence layer on top of the existing Temo Letlotlo Crop Management System (CMS). It turns the farmer data the Ministry already collects into farmer-level decisions and program-level insight — helping move farmers from subsistence to commercial production. Farmers and extension officers use it free; the Ministry funds it.

## The opportunity

Temo Letlotlo has registered 100,000+ farmers and aims to commercialise smallholder production and build self-reliance. The CMS captures registration and administrative data well — but it does not yet turn that data into the decisions a commercialising farmer needs (what to plant, what it costs, whether they are profitable), or the analytics the Ministry needs to target support and measure outcomes. Commercialisation needs an intelligence layer on top of the registry, not another registry.

## The solution — AgroEdge

AgroEdge plugs into CMS farmer data and adds:

- **Activity, expense and income logging** — a simple record per farmer, captured with extension-officer support.
- **Dashboards & AI insights** — cost optimisation, profitability analysis, productivity recommendations, and alerts.
- **Credit / loan-readiness scoring** — turning the farm record into a trusted data trail that can connect farmers to the Agricultural Credit Guarantee Scheme and the National Development Bank.

It complements the CMS; it does not replace it.

## Why this fits Botswana now

- Directly serves Temo Letlotlo's output-based, wealth-creation, self-reliance objectives.
- Botswana funds its own agriculture programs — no donor dependency or external timelines.
- Gives the current administration a concrete way to show the program delivers commercial results.

## The model

The Ministry (or program) funds the service; farmers and extension officers use it at no cost. AgroEdge is the vendor; the Ministry is the customer; the farmer is the beneficiary.
