# Beachhead Markets

## Botswana — primary beachhead
Government subsidy programme: **Lemang Dijo** (launched Nov/Dec 2025) — current live programme.
- P220M budget, 100% subsidy on tillage/pesticides up to 1 hectare via a P4,400 e-voucher
- Applications via the Crop Management System
- Replaced **Temo Letlotlo** (2023–2025), which itself replaced ISPAAD. Botswana cycles these subsidy programmes roughly every 2 years.
- Temo Letlotlo can still be cited as historical/legacy context (e.g. dashboard demo panels) but must be clearly dated as historical, not current.
- ⚠️ Investor materials, ministry concept notes, and root CLAUDE.md were written around Temo Letlotlo and need updating to reference Lemang Dijo.

## Zimbabwe — secondary market
Pfumvudza/Intwasa programme + FAO IDEA registry. Concept note and meeting request prepared for the Ministry of Agriculture.

## Kenya — secondary market
KIAMIS registry. Built by FAO + Kenya's Ministry of Agriculture, funded by Sweden, handed to government November 2025 — confirms governments fund and own this kind of infrastructure. FAO is both a potential channel partner and a strategic risk.
