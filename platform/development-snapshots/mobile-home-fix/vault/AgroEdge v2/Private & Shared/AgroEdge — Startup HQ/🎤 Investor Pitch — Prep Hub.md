## The narrative arc (the spine of the pitch)

1. **Hook** — African farms run blind: no records, no data, no leverage.
2. **Problem** — that blindness costs farmers credit, yield, and fair prices.
3. **The insight** — the farmers who feel it can't pay to fix it. The payers are institutions.
4. **Solution** — AgroEdge: an AI farm-intelligence layer. For farmers, decisions. For institutions, data + de-risking.
5. **Model** — B2G. Governments and national agricultural programmes pay; farmers use it free. (Lenders become a second buyer later via the data → credit-scoring flywheel.)
6. **Go-to-market** — ride government farmer registries. Botswana + Zimbabwe concept notes already drafted. **Policy fit (Zimbabwe Vision 2030):** the national strategy names agriculture as the GDP engine (targets: ~18% of GDP, treble agri-trade, halve poverty by 2030) and explicitly prioritises ICT-driven farmer intelligence — even naming an "Agriculture Information Management System." We're not pitching a new idea; we execute a commitment the state has already made, riding the Pfumvudza/Intwasa channel (1.5M smallholders in 2024).
7. **Market** — tens of millions of farmers, billions in losses, a proven B2G template (Kenya).
8. **Why we win** — we don't rebuild the registry; we're the intelligence layer on top, with a data → credit-scoring flywheel.
9. **Team** — product + finance (Jaydeane) and AI + engineering (Aone).
10. **The ask** — [amount] to close pilots and ship.
