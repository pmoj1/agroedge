# Product Roadmap

## MVP Scope (v1)

> **Goal:** Let a farmer log their activities, expenses, and income — and receive one AI insight per week about their farm performance.
>

## Milestones

- 🧱 MVP built — core logging + one AI insight working
- 🌱 Pilot launch — 10 farmers onboarded, 4-week pilot
- 📊 Pilot review — analyse data and gather feedback
- 🚀 Public launch — open to 100+ farmers
- 💰 Seed raise — first funding round
