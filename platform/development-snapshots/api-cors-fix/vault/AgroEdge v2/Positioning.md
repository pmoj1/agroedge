# Positioning

## Core model
B2G — institutions (governments, NGOs, development funders) pay per enrolled farmer per season. Farmers access the platform free.

AgroEdge is not a farm app — it's the intelligence and decision-support layer sitting on top of government registries.

> Temo CMS tells you who your farmers are. AgroEdge tells you what to do about it.

## The defensible lane
The institutional intelligence layer is entirely unoccupied in target markets. Competing as "another AI farm app" is the wrong lane. The farmer-facing AI agronomist is the engagement hook; the institutional dashboard is the actual product and revenue model.

## No-overclaiming rule (standing principle)
- Government relationships = outreach in progress, not signed
- Institutional dashboard = planned, not built
- Platform = working MVP, not live in production
- No LOIs or contracts claimed as executed
