Why this works for rollout
Selling the AI service to farmers, not the app

No install friction

Farmers already have WhatsApp. Onboarding is "save this number," not an app store download and account creation.

Voice-first data entry

Low-literacy farmers speak instead of typing forms — the intake agent transcribes and structures it.

Always-on agronomist

Photo a diseased leaf, get a diagnosis grounded in that farmer's own planting and weather history — not generic advice.

Same backend, new channel

WhatsApp is a channel layer in front of the existing farm-records API and database — the mobile app remains the deeper dashboard for farmers who want it.
