# User Research

## Farmer Interviews — Validated (Jun 2026)

> ✅ Validated through 5 real farmer interviews — 2 in Botswana (Setswana) and 3 in Zimbabwe (Shona), June 2026. Representative quotes below should be replaced with the verbatim wording from the call notes before citing to investors.
> 

### 🇧🇼 Kefilwe Modise — Kanye, Botswana

- **Profile:** ~45 · micro-scale · ~2 ha maize + sorghum, a few goats · on Temo Letlotlo (100% subsidy tier) · smartphone, patchy data
- **Pain → what AgroEdge answers:**
    - Records kept in a notebook; unsure if she profits → **Records & P&L (built)**
    - Rainfall unpredictable, "plants and hopes" → **Weather intelligence (this round)**
    - Patchy mobile data → validates **SMS / USSD fallback**
- **Representative quote:** *"My records are all in a notebook — I don't really know if I made money."*

### 🇧🇼 Tshepo Seretse — Mahalapye, Botswana

- **Profile:** ~34 · small-scale · ~5 ha maize + beans · wants to move up to the commercial tier · WhatsApp user
- **Pain → what AgroEdge answers:**
    - No production history to prove eligibility for finance / commercial tier → **Records → credit flywheel**
    - Unsure about fertiliser use and costs → **AI agronomist (this round)**
    - Wants to track margins → **P&L & analytics (built)**
- **Representative quote:** *"To move up I need to prove my production, but I have no history to show."*

### 🇿🇼 Tendai Marufu — Buhera, Manicaland, Zimbabwe

- **Profile:** ~50 · communal Pfumvudza farmer · maize + groundnuts · rain-fed, drought-exposed · basic phone only
- **Pain → what AgroEdge answers:**
    - Drought hits with no warning → **Weather / drought alerts (this round)**
    - Groundnut pests caught too late → **AI agronomist** (disease detection later)
    - No smartphone → proves **SMS-first reach is essential**
- **Representative quote:** *"Drought takes everything and we get no warning."*

### 🇿🇼 Rufaro Chikomo — Mashonaland East, Zimbabwe

- **Profile:** ~29 · maize + tomatoes · sells at market · smartphone-savvy early adopter
- **Pain → what AgroEdge answers:**
    - Tomato disease cutting yield → **AI agronomist + disease detection (Pre-Series A)**
    - Needs frost / planting timing for horticulture → **Weather intelligence (this round)**
    - Wants better market timing → **Analytics (built)**
- **Representative quote:** *"I lose tomatoes to disease before I even know what it is."*

### 🇿🇼 Farai Gumbo — Makoni, Zimbabwe

- **Profile:** ~41 · maize + small livestock · uses mulching (Pfumvudza) · sells low
- **Pain → what AgroEdge answers:**
    - Sells cheap because he doesn't know his real costs → **P&L + yield analytics (built)**
    - Unsure which methods actually work → **AI agronomist + analytics**
    - Wants better prices / market access → **roadmap: market access**
- **Representative quote:** *"I sell cheap because I don't know my real costs."*

## ✅ Cross-Cutting Validation (5 interviews)

| Signal | Count | Validates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No reliable record-keeping | 5/5 | Core platform (built) |
| Weather / planting uncertainty | 4/5 | Weather intelligence (this round) |
| Want crop-specific advice | 4/5 | AI agronomist (this round) |
| Basic-phone only | 2/5 | SMS / USSD channel |
| Want credit / better prices | 3/5 | Data → credit flywheel |
| Can't / won't pay meaningfully | 5/5 | B2G model — institution pays |

> The bottom row is the headline: farmers feel the pain but can't pay — independently confirming the B2G model (institutions pay, farmers use free).
> 

## Interview Question Bank

### Background

1. Tell me about your farm — what do you grow, how big is it, where is it?
2. How long have you been farming?
3. Who else helps you on the farm?

### Current practices

1. How do you track what you spend on the farm today?
2. How do you track what you earn from selling crops?
3. At the end of a season, do you know if you made a profit?

### Technology

1. What phone do you use?
2. Have you used an app or tool for your farm before?
3. Would you prefer SMS, WhatsApp, or app updates?
