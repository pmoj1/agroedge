# Key Learnings & Principles

## Kurima Mari failure mode
FAO Zimbabwe tool (~12k installs) stalled when donor funding ended. AgroEdge's recurring commercial contract model is the explicit counter-narrative — governments pay per season, not a one-off donor grant.

## Data protection architecture
Kenya, Zimbabwe, and Botswana all restrict cross-border data transfer. This supports a two-stage architecture: Bubble for pilot, government-grade re-platform at Pre-Series A.

## Rebuild timing
Committing to a full custom rebuild before a signed pilot contract exists risks burning pre-seed capital against an unproven assumption. Staged approach is strategically sound.

## Investor messaging
Sector-fit matters, but credible investors as door-openers have value even without direct fit. Concise prose beats heavily formatted output for investor communications.
